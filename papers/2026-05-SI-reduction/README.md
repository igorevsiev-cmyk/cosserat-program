## Abstract (one paragraph)

A two-channel Cosserat-continuum interpretation of electromagnetism and gravitation in a single elastic medium. Four identifications of medium parameters with fundamental constants of nature (P1: `ρ ≡ μ₀`; P2: `G_shear ≡ 1/ε₀`; P3: `ℏ` as the action quantum of microrotations; P4: `G` via the Kleinert gauge mechanism) reduce the SI base `{m, kg, s, A}` to a single energy dimension. Combined with the hopfionic identification of the electron, geometry yields a bare fine-structure constant `α_bare = 1/128 = 2⁻⁷`, agreeing with the measured `α(M_Z) = 1/128.9` to within 0.7%; the QED running to `1/137` is the standard vacuum-polarization shift.

- Canonical set of fundamental constants reduces to `{ε₀, μ₀, ℏ, G}`.
- Speed of light `c = 1/√(ε₀μ₀)` arises as the transverse Cosserat-wave speed.
- Charge as the cross-sectional area of a defect: `[Q] ↔ [m²]`.
- Planck length is derived, not fundamental: `l_P = l₀²/(2√2 · l_G)`.
- Fine-structure constant: `α_bare = 1/128 = 2⁻⁷`; QED renormalization recovers the low-energy `1/137`.
- Linearized Cosserat metric reproduces all five classical tests of General Relativity.
